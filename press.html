About Pip Puzzle

Selected for EVO 2025+2026's Indie Game Devs Area, Pip Puzzle is a competitive puzzle fighter where matching gems with Trigger Gems destroys them and floods your opponent's board with junk. Choose a character with a unique skill; from turning the screen black and white to dropping steel balls that destroy opponents' valuable gems—and battle through stages that evolve as the match unfolds. Stages evolve visually and audibly as a match unfolds, with a Sega Genesis-style score that evolves into jazz, drum and bass, rock, etc. depending on who's losing. Play Arcade, Story, or Zen mode; solo, or against friends online and locally.

  • ModdingCustom characters (inc. skills via dedicated language) & stages
